## Rules
- UI alert words: Normal, Waspada, Amaran, Bahaya. Never name any agency or charity in the game. Official level names beyond these are unverified.
- Run ends about 71.6s (play stops 63s, boat docks 68s, he boards by himself 5s later if nobody taps, T.wear 0.9, T.sail 2). Timeline is the old one x0.9 (owner: too relaxed). No deaths. No ads. No donation link. Footer: Projek kreatif, bukan produk rasmi.
- Pass: first tap <3s throttled 4G; p5 FPS 30+; 4/5 testers finish unaided (auto-board fixes the idle case); wording matches sources; link preview renders.

## Decisions (all tunable in data.js)
- Layout: Serambi, Ruang Tamu, Dapur in a row at floor heights 1.0/1.6/1.3 (flood order per design); Bilik above Ruang Tamu; Para deck above Bilik. Upper floors see-through.
- Never stuck: a level seals when a floating head is within 0.7 of its ceiling (ground about 47s, Bilik about 58s); taps there are refused and the water carries him up.
- Tap empty slot = put down the last item picked up. Tap Para or docked boat = put down everything. Banked items stay banked. Picking = nearest target centre on screen. Ending: the water clock stops play at 70s (T.max); he takes what he holds to the Para and waves both arms for help (no toast text); the boat shows at 72s, docks at 75s and waits; the boatman's jacket bubble (icon only) pops above the driver. Tapping the bubble or the boat boards him: walks to the dock, what he holds goes on the boat, jacket on, boards 0.9s later (T.wear), boat leaves, run ends 2s later (T.sail). No tap, no boarding: he waits safe on the Para. Tests and bots tap the boat as soon as it docks.
- Cat (no-deaths rule, owner OK): bolts once or twice when reached; runs the real route (doors, stairs, ladder) ahead of the water, ends on the roof beam and rides the boat unscored. Only a carried cat scores.
- Messages: 6 per run, one hoax in Amaran, burst of 3 in Bahaya. An open message blocks taps on the house for up to 4s; Sebarkan costs 3s, Semak sumber rasmi is free. NEEDS PLAYTEST.
- Floating items wander every 4 to 9s. Slosh dropped as redundant. Tuning (owner asked faster): speed 2.6, grab 0.25/0.4/0.65s, speed scale 1 to 10, 10 = empty hands on a dry floor (2.6 tiles/s): hands in use 10 / 8.6 / 7.2 (TUNE.carry, a weight 3 item takes both), stairs and ladder x0.7 (TUNE.climb, one value) so empty 7, one item 6, both hands 5; nothing below TUNE.floor 0.5 (5), wading included. Weight 2 items no longer slow him extra. Owner wanted it easier: balance caps loosened (best mean 97, casual 85); yard dry through Normal.
- Look (Stage 7): village and house are built in code; furniture, pak cik, rescuer, cat, boat, fridge, TV and radio are Kenney models baked by tools/bake.mjs (no loader in the game; models.bin, 48KB gzipped, loads after Mula is enabled; box models stay if it fails). Pak cik keeps a code-built kain pelikat, plus a white baju (baju() recolours the baked green shirt) and a cream songkok (SONGKOK, fitted to the baked head). About 39 draw calls before the art pass, one shadow light. view.look(x, y, z, width) is a close-up for art checks.
- Flood look (art pass): one opaque, unlit light-mud plane (C.water) floods the whole village, so no dry ground stays in frame at Amaran and Bahaya; streaks and rings only near the house (SW in render.js). Alert dimming cut to hemi -0.12, sun -0.15 (was -0.45, -0.6). Bilik floor glass 0.8 (was 0.6). Cream rim (back-face copy, 0.045) on the man, boat and carried items.
- Slots: each has at = [dx, dz, lift] in data.js. The item is drawn on furniture beside the walking spot, and the water must rise past the lift before it floats (counters 0.67, tikar 0.03). Fridge and TV have a home slot (k3, t1). k1 and k2 moved to z 0.95 to stand in front of the counters. Landing = village with a gentle sway; Mula zooms to the house while roof and front walls fade. Tall scenery is kept out of the strip between camera and house (open() in models.js). Village (7h, models.js): main house shell in weathered boards, red zinc gables (batch.zinc), lean-to serambi, concrete footings. 24 neighbours from kampung() in 4 types (panjang, cross, patch with side serambi, limas with batch.hip and X rails), spread with yards between: near side, three loose rows across the river on a bank path and two back lanes, titian footbridge, laterite paths, bands across the road. Seeded trees (fruit trees, palms, banana clumps, 2.6 apart) plus framing palms; no trunk or crown over the river or road; tall things pass open() (out of the play view in front of the house). Nothing is built off screen or under Mula (seen()). Mobile: village batch is open (unturned boxes keep only +x/+y/+z faces, shadowSide FrontSide); boards, pickets, leaves and grass are flat faces. Village 33k triangles; headless landing 91k with shadows, play 49k. Occlusion (keeps depth when the slow-phone guard drops shadows): baked into vertex colours (batch.ao: foot of ground boxes, back walls, floor strip along the back wall, bottom and top board rows) plus one contact-shadow mesh (blobMesh: soft quad per house, tree, bush; strength in the vertex colour, read as alpha). Draw calls 83 at landing (+1).
- UI: chunky outlined buttons and badges, Fredoka font (OFL) with rounded system fallback. Messages look like a chat app, no logo or app name. Sound: audio.js synthesizes everything first (music stays synth); recorded files replace rain, water, thunder, steps, cat, splash, crickets, UI sounds and the win jingle when they load (FILES, BEDS, REC tables; gains set from measured loudness). Mute button remembered. Credits: CREDITS.md and the Kredit panel.
- Titles: awal; lengkap = every kit item that spawned is safe (6+ spawn); dokumen = Dokumen plus every Ubat/Wang present; comot (hidden cat, no hint anywhere); bekal (Air, Makanan, Pakaian); isyarat (Lampu, Radio, Bateri, Wisel; 2+ spawned); peti; tabah. No toast text for the boat (horn only).
- Card tip is "Kalau diarah pindah, terus pindah. Jangan tunggu air naik." (sourced, owner OK). Language: modern casual Bahasa Malaysia, never Indonesian.
- Kongsi: Web Share with the PNG if allowed, else link only, else Salin pautan + Simpan gambar (<a download>) + long-press on the card image. If share.js cannot load, the end screen shows the plain score and Main Semula.

## Measurements
- Load (Slow 4G, cold, median of 5), headless on a dev box, 2026-11-24: 2429ms (4x CPU 3132ms), 182KB over 7 requests before Mula; earlier on the live site 2304ms (2699ms 4x CPU). Pass. Three.js self hosted and tree-shaken. models.bin (48KB gz) and sounds (671KB) load after Mula.
- Slow-phone guard: 65% resolution and no shadows if frames 20 to 140 average under 40 fps (tested by forcing 25 fps; it also fires in software GL: 81 to 54 draw calls, 88k to 47k triangles). Not handled: WebGL context loss.
- Balance, 100 seeds (2026-11-24): zero taps 0 (clock never starts); minimal effort (one tap on Dokumen) 10 in 100%; casual bot 66% of nilai; best bot (0.25s reaction) 91%, saves every item 0%, every non-decoy 55%; clears every non-decoy at 2.5x speed. Run length 71.6s.
- Sources (2026-11-16): 10 kit items and the tip match the DOSM-hosted article; alert words match the flood portal legend; Ready2go link is 404 (UNVERIFIED); "bersedia berpindah" has a news report only.
- Frame (software GL, 394x718): village is split into 24-tile chunks (culled; only chunks near the house cast shadows). Draw calls 100 landing, 98 to 107 play (was 83 to 86 on a different count, see audit.md). Pixel ratio capped 1.5. Flood contrast (p95+0.05)/(p5+0.05) in the house box with the dark halo: Amaran 40s 3.3, Bahaya 64s 2.6 (was 1.61; still under 4:1, guard was on). Details: docs/audit.md.
