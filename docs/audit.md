Flood readability: contrast = (p95 + 0.05) / (p5 + 0.05) of relative luminance inside the screen box of the house (x 0..9, y 0..6.6, z 0..3), HUD hidden. Target 4:1.

The Bahaya house box matches the 1.62 in NOTES (1.61 at 394). It fails 4:1 from 53 s on, and 40 s is borderline. Cause: the water (relative luminance p5 about 0.31) is nearly as light as the lit wood (p95 about 0.54 to 0.59), so only a water tone at or under about 0.10 reaches 4:1 on this box (p95 0.54 needs p5 of 0.0975 or less). The 0.07 tone tried earlier (7.3:1) was rejected as too dark. The loft box (Bilik and Para, with white sheets) passes at most times but is 3.7 to 4.2 at 64 s. The earlier NOTES figure for the loft (5.15) could not be reproduced exactly; these are fixed-time readings from one seeded run.

## 5. Critique, ranked (most first)
1. **The run never ends unless he taps an emoji-only bubble.** Evidence: sim 400 s after one tap, then idle: `done false`, boat docked, score 10; same for a casual bot that never boards (sim.js sets `endAt` only after a board tap; design.md says no early departure). A tester who misses the bubble sits on the Para forever, which also breaks the "4 of 5 finish unaided" line. Fix: auto-board 6 to 8 s after docking and add the word "Naik bot" on the bubble. Effort S (one tuning value in `T`, one string, a CSS tweak).
2. **The jacket bubble covers the man and what he holds** at the one moment the player must read the screen (76 s shots at all three sizes). Fix: move it above the boat driver's head by 40 to 60 px, or to the side of the boat. Effort S (`driverAt` offset in render.js).
3. **Flood readability fails 4:1 at Bahaya (1.61) and is borderline at Amaran (3.8 to 4.0).** Evidence: table in section 3. Push back on the metric: water luminance alone cannot reach 4:1 on a box dominated by light wood. Fix: judge the loft, the man and the items (3.7 to 5.0 now), and add a dark contact line or a dark rim on the deck and the roof beam so the silhouette separates from the water. Effort M.
4. **Silent ending.** No sound at the wave (70 s), bubble, jacket-on or boarding. Fix: reuse `ping` for the bubble, `good` for jacket-on, `click` for boarding, a short existing take for the wave. Effort S (index.html `sounds()`).
5. **Mix: synthesized alert, horn and whoosh sit 5 to 15 dB under the recorded effects, and nothing limits the master.** Evidence: section 4. Fix: add a DynamicsCompressor on `out` and raise the synth gains by about 8 dB. Effort S. Owner to check on the Redmi.
6. **Ogg Vorbis on iPhone is untested.** Evidence: 18 files are Vorbis. The synth takes over if decode fails, so nothing breaks, but iPhone players get a different game. Fix: test on an iPhone; if it fails, ship MP3 or AAC twins. Effort M.
7. **The village is one 32.6k-triangle mesh drawn in both passes, so a slow phone loses all shadows at once.** Fix: split it in 3 to 4 chunks (cull what is off screen), cast shadows only from the near ones. Do it only if p5 FPS on the Redmi is under 30. Effort M.
8. **Small targets and text.** Empty-slot tap area 41 px at 360x640; chat card text 11 px; the "bersedia berpindah" tag is small. Fix: slot reach 0.6 to 0.65 tiles, 12 px minimum, tag 13 px with a dark pill. Effort S.
9. **Difficulty may be too flat after the easier pass.** Evidence (balance.mjs): minimal effort 10 points, casual bot 68% of nilai, best bot 93% and every non-decoy saved in 85% of seeds (it was 7% before). Saving everything stays at 0%. Fix: watch the 6 testers first; if replays feel flat, move `T.levels` or the grab times, not speeds. Effort S (data.js).
10. **Audio weight.** rain.mp3 216 KB, splash.wav 79 KB, frog.wav 26 KB are the heaviest sound files and load after the first tap. Fix: re-encode the two WAVs (about 90 KB saved) after the owner approves the swap. Effort S.
